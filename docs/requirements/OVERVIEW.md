Remote connection

synchronization







Compare local or remote source and destination folders to identify matching, modifying, creating, and deleting files.

Simply click the mouse to synchronize them unidirectionally or bidirectionally, or save them as favorites.

The analysis speed is 20 times faster than ForkLift 3.
